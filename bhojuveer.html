THE SANCTUARY

Refined, contemporary, aesthetic, and garden-inspired.

Nestled away in Sikraul, Bhojuveer, this outlet was envisioned as a tranquil oasis from Varanasi's bustling heart. Hand-finished limestone walls, natural light through glass ceilings, and lush tropical botanicals set the stage for mindful morning coffees, intimate dinners, and poetic afternoons.
