Priyank Mehta

“The café had a great ambience and overall vibe. It is divided into three distinct sections: one dedicated purely to breakfast hours, another being an open area with a few tables and a spacious dance floor, and a third, small and compact section located on the first floor. The open-area tables, which offer the best vibe, cost around ₹2,000 per person and include a buffet. If you choose to order from the à la carte menu, you are asked to sit on the first floor, which unfortunately lacks the same atmosphere. The food, overall, was below average. We ordered Manchow soup, which surprisingly included mushrooms and paneer along with the usual vegetables. The honey chilli potatoes were extremely hard and difficult to eat. We also ordered Schezwan fried rice with Manchurian gravy, which was decent. The only standout dish of the evening was the tandoori paneer tikka starter—spicy, soft, and creamy—served with masala onions and curd-based pudina chutney.”

Sanskar Jaiswal

“The vibe and ambience of this place is just superb. I think that's the main reason why this place is so hyped. The service was also fast. alka verma
